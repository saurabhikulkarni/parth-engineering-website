Decades of Excellence

Over 35 years of engineering expertise in this field.

At Parth Engineering, we transform engineering expertise into innovative thermal solutions that power critical industries. From Naval and Power applications to Sugar, Distillery, Chemical, and Steel plants, we design and manufacture high-performance Heat Exchangers, Air Coolers, Radiators, Condensers, Chillers, Reboilers, and Hot Air Systems built for absolute reliability and efficiency.

Driven by quality, precision, and continuous innovation, we are committed to delivering durable, energy-efficient equipment that helps our customers achieve greater performance, productivity, and operational excellence.

In our fully equipped Nashik workshop, we oversee every stage of production. From automatic tube-to-fin mechanical expansions and certified ASME welding to advanced radiographic inspections and leak testing at 1.5x design pressure, our thermal solutions are built to withstand the most demanding industrial runtimes.
